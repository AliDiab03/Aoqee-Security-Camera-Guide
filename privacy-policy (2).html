Privacy Policy

Aoqee Security Camera Guide · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aoqee Security Camera Guide Android app (the “App”) handles information. The App is a setup and troubleshooting guide for security cameras. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

In short: the App does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, phone number or location, and does not access your camera, microphone, photos or contacts. The only data collected is by Google AdMob, which we use to show ads, and only according to your consent choices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the App has no sign-up or login).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your photos, videos, files, contacts, microphone or camera. The App does not request these permissions.
  • Any video or footage from your security cameras. The App is a guide only and does not connect to your cameras.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of data locally on your device, for example your progress through the setup guide and your language preference. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is not included in cloud backups. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

The Storage Calculator tool performs its calculations on your device. The values you enter are not transmitted or saved by us.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. The App may show banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads and app-open ads.

When ads are requested, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (the App declares the AD_ID permission)
  • IP address and approximate region derived from it
  • Device and OS information (model, OS version, language, screen details)
  • App usage and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, to personalize ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive your personal data from Google. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your Consent Choices

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and certain US states. Ads are requested only after the consent flow allows it. You can change your choices where the consent form offers that option.

You can also control ad personalization from your device:

  • Android settings: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Opting out does not remove ads. It makes them less relevant to you.

5. Internet Access and External Links

The App uses the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to load ads and web pages, and to detect whether you are online. Some content in the App links to external websites, such as search results, maps or online stores. These links open inside the App’s built-in browser. Those websites are operated by third parties and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their own policies. Downloads from these pages are disabled, and the App does not open other apps from them.

6. Data Security

The App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) for web content and ads, and does not allow unencrypted traffic. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not hold any personal information that could be exposed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not hold personal data about you, most such requests relate to data held by Google as an advertising provider, which you can manage through your Google account and device ad settings.
